#About Me

My name is Levi Elliott, I transferred to UNC Charlotte to further my education in Mechanical Engineering. I chose the Mechanical Engineering pathway due to my interests of how everything works together to reach a certain outcome. This started from a young age. I have raced dirt bikes since I was 8 years old. Racing is not possible with out maintenance. My Dad and I have spent hundreds of hours in the shop replacing parts, fixing unknown problems, and taking the dirt bikes apart. This taught me a lot in life. The better you take care of a machine the longer it lasts. If you push something to the limit there is always a failure point. Then comes finding a stronger and lighter solution. As an Engineer I am passionate about my designs and strive to find better solutions to problems.

To me, a Engineering decision must include why I chose my end solution. Then how I supported my solution with evidence and work to show my conclusions that I made. I will consider my trade-offs from one solution to another. I will be determined to solve problems. I will always strive to improve efficiency and the long Gevity of a product. Failure is not the end, it is knowledge to improve and learn from your mistakes.
